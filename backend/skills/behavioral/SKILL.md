# Behavioral & Leadership Interview Evaluation Rubric

## Core Philosophy & Bar-Raiser Expectations
Behavioral interviews test **past behavior as the strongest predictor of future performance**. Top candidates deliver tight, memorable STAR stories with unmistakable **first-person ownership ("I")**, clear **decision criteria**, **quantified results**, and **honest self-reflection**.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Personal Ownership & Agency ("I" vs "We") (30%)
- Clearly distinguishes personal actions ("I analyzed...", "I wrote the RFC...", "I pushed back...") from team/manager actions.
- Takes direct accountability for misses, misalignments, or failures without finger-pointing.

### 2. STAR Structure & Pacing (25%)
- **Situation & Task (<=20%)**: Concise context, stakes, and constraints—no 3-minute preamble.
- **Action (>=60%)**: Step-by-step reasoning, alternatives rejected, stakeholder alignment, and decisive execution.
- **Result & Reflection (~20%)**: Concrete business/customer metrics, before/after baselines, and transferable lessons.

### 3. Scope, Complexity & Judgment (25%)
- Demonstrates navigating genuine ambiguity, cross-functional conflict, resource trade-offs, or skeptical leadership.
- Explains *why* an action was chosen over reasonable alternatives.

### 4. Communication & Coachability (20%)
- Direct answers to follow-up probes (does not evade "what would you do differently?").
- Balanced talk ratio (~60–70% candidate during storytelling, concise check-ins rather than unbroken monologues), minimal hedging ("maybe", "kind of", "I guess").

## Red Flags
- Persistent **"we" masking** when asked about individual contribution.
- Storytelling that stays at 30,000 feet ("we collaborated and launched it") with zero concrete actions or metrics.
- Fake weaknesses / humblebrags ("I work too hard") or blaming others for failures.
- Rambling setup with >150-word uninterrupted backstory before reaching the core problem.
